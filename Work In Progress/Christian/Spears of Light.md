*Flavor Text*

<br>

**Effect:**
You conjure spears made out of pure light hovering behind you with their tips pointed upwards.
The amount of spears conjured is equal to your spell casting stat.

The spears each have the [[Lumination| Lumination(5 meters)]] Trait.

**System:**
For as long as this spell is active you gain the following Actions:

- Throw (Spear of Light)
- [Transfer Spell (Spear of Light)](app://obsidian.md/Transfer%20Spell)
- [Luminescent Detonation (Spear of Light)](app://obsidian.md/Luminescent%20Detonation)

**Throw (Spear of Light):**
**Range:** Same as a spear plus spell stat.
You magically propel one of the conjured spears still floating behind you, at a target within range.
If the spear hits the target, it deals damage, plus your spell stat, and effects the target as if hit by a [spear](obsidian://open?vault=Emulate&file=Content%2FGear%2FWeapons%2FSpear). The spear sticks to the hit target and emits light as normal and can later be used for [Luminescent Detonation (Spear of Light)](app://obsidian.md/Luminescent%20Detonation).
If the spear missed the target, it lands somewhere near the target, as determined by the GM, and can later be used for [Luminescent Detonation (Spear of Light)](app://obsidian.md/Luminescent%20Detonation).

When using Transfer Spell (Spear of Light) or Luminescent Detonation (Spear of Light), you  choose any of the conjured spears individually.
