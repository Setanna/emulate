Windslash

*You slice the air with such force or technique that your sword sends out a slash of wind strong enough to cut flesh*

Requirements: +4 Strength or +4 Dex

This talent can only be used with slashing weapons.
You can make a Swing for the Fences attack against a target beyond your weapons normal reach, up to a maximum distance of your Dex or Str in meters.
The damage of this attack is equal to your weapons damage die (You do not add Str or Dex to this damage)
