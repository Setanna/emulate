# Freezing
*The cold is patient. It wins by staying*

> [!info|right] Die Steps
> | Die  | Step |
>|:---:|:----:|
>| d4  |  1   |
>| d6  |  2   |
>| d8  |  3   |
>| d10 |  4   |
>| d12 |  5   |



**System:**
While you have the Freezing Condition you gain the following effects:
- You take a [[Circumstance]] Penalty on
	- Attacks
	- Physical Stat Based Skill Checks
	- Reflex Saving Throws
- You reduce your Speed by your Freezing Condition's die step (Minimum 1)

The [[Circumstance]] Penalty is equal to your Freezing Condition's value.

If your Freezing Condition value would reach a d12 or higher, you freeze solid. For as long as your Freezing Condition is a d12 or higher, you gain the [[Unconscious]] and [[Paralyzed]] Conditions.
