**Contracting the Disease:**
At the end of each turn a creature heard another creature with Whisperpox talk within 6 meters, they must succeed a Fortitude DC or contract the DC.

<br>

**Curing the Disease:**

### Stage 1
*A raspy voice and aching throat*

<br>

You take a -1 [[Disease]] Penalty to all Rolls and DC's for Actions with the [[Verbal]] Trait.
