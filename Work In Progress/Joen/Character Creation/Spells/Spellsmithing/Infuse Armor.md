___

**System:**
When you cast this spell, choose on of the Spells required by this Spell that you have learnt, you infuse the spell into a piece of armor.

<br>

Any creature grappled by, or any creature that grapples the targeted creature takes damage damage at the start of their turn, and they reduce all damage with the same Damage Type as the spell equal to your Spell Stat.
