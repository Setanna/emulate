The tempest is born as your hands collide in a crack of thunder. You create a 2 meter sphere that blasts away any creatures and effects. Fog and Gas gets dispersed in a 2 meter area around you, if they have a longer duration than a round they return after a round.

<br>

**Success:** The Defender takes half damage.
**Fail:** The Defender takes full damage.
**Critical Fail:** The Defender takes full damage is [[Stunned | Stunned 1]].
