<h1> Raise Shield <span style="margin-left: auto;">  </span> </h1>

[[Rules/Traits/Item| Item (Shield)]]{.trait}

[[Reactive]]{.trait}
 
<div style="clear:both" />

**Reaction Trigger:** You or an ally within reach is the target of a Weapon Attack and you're using your shield reactively.
___

**System:**
You lift your shield to block the attack. Until the start of your next turn you add your **Shield Value** to your **Armor Value**.

<br>

When Raising a Shield for your ally, the effect lasts until they leave your reach or the start of their next turn, and the **Shield Value** is added to their **Armor Value**.

<br>

Alternatively you can use an Second on your own turn instead of a Split Second.
