<h1> Cracked Form <span style="margin-left: auto;">1 XP</span> </h1>

[[Resonance]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** Dexterity 1+
___
*Even stone must crack to let the wind pass through*

<br>

**System:**
Whenever you would take a [[Circumstance]] Penalty Die from the [[Petrified]] Condition on your Dexterity Based Attacks, Skills or Saving Throws, reduce the die by one step.

<br>

Additionally you reduce the Armor Value gained from [[Petrified]] by one step.

<br>

**Resonance:**
You reduce the die by one additional step for each talent you have with Cracked Form as a requirement.
