<h1> Boulder's Momentum <span style="margin-left: auto;">2 XP</span> </h1>

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** Strength 2+, [[Stoneclad Form]]
___
*Like a rolling boulder, your momentum forces foes aside with unstoppable force.*

<br>

**System:**
Whenever you would move at least 5 meters in a straight line before making a Melee [[Making a Strike | Strike]]. If you hit the Attack, you can also target the creature with a [[Shove]] Maneuver.
