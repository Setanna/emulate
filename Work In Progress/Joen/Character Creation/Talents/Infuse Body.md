<h1> Infuse Body <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 2 XP</span> </h1>

[[Arcane]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** Ability to cast [[Scorch]] or [[Shock]]
___
**

<br>

**System:**
Whenever you cast one of the Spells that are Required of this Talent, you can increase the Action Cost for the spell by one and channel the energy into your body. 

<br>

For as long as the spell is active, instead of its regular effects, any creature you grapple, or grapples you take damage damage at the start of their turn, and you reduce all damage with the same Damage Type as the spell equal to your Spell Stat.
