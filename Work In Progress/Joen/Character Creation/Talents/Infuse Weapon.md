<h1> Infuse Weapon <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 2 XP</span> </h1>

[[Arcane]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** Ability to cast [[Scorch]] or [[Shock]]
___
*A sword is good. A sword on fire? Better.*

<br>

**System:**
Whenever you cast one of the Spells that are Required of this Talent, you can increase the Action Cost for the spell by one and channel the energy into a weapon. 

<br>

For as long as the spell is active, instead of its regular effects, you apply the Spell Damage to the weapon and the weapon gains the respective elemental Trait.
