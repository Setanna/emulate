**System:**
Whenever you would fail an Attack; Saving Throw; or Skill Roll, you gain a +1 [[Morale]] Bonus to the specific roll, up to a maximum of your Wisdom. This [[Morale]] Bonus is reset to 0 whenever you would succeed a roll; start or end an encounter; or start a new task for a Skill Roll.
