<h1> Mirrorbane <span style="margin-left: auto;">4</span> </h1>


<div style="clear:both" /> 

___
*Even illusions refuse to wear your likeness.*

<br>

**System:**
You gain the [[Ugly]] and [[Hideous]] Characteristics. While cursed, you lose the benefits of the [[Pretty]] and [[Beautiful]] Characteristics.

<br>

Additionally any reflective surface, that would reflect your visage. Cracks, clouds or ripples to prevent you reflection from being visible.
