# Duration
The duration of a spell is defined as how long the spell lasts. Noted below is all the possible durations of a spell:
- **Instantaneous**: The spell's effects occur once
- **Until End of Encounter***: The spell's effect last until the end of the encounter
- **Undtil End of Next Preparation**: The spell's effect last until the end of the next preparation
- **Permanent**: The spell's effect is permanent
