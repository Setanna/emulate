# Range
The range of a spell is defined as how far it can reach from the caster. Noted below is all the possible ranges of a spell:
- **Personal**: Range is limited to the caster
- **Touch**: Range is limited to the caster's reach
- **Horizon**: Range is limited to the visible horizon
- **Astral**: Range is limited to the local solar system
- **Unlimited**: Range is unlimited
