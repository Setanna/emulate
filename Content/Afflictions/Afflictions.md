# Afflictions
Afflictions can vary from curses to a limp. Any character can gain an Affliction during their escapades, but some can start with them from birth.

## Starting with an Affliction
To start with an Affliction, simply apply the Affliction the the character, and increase the XP of said Character by the level of the Affliction times five. Some Afflictions have varied levels, when choosing the Affliction you can chose what level you want the Affliction at. Diseases start at Stage 1, but you can choose to start it at any Stage you want.

### Curses
Evil Witches or wishes gone wrong—a curse can be the result of many things, but always stems from magic. A Curse is build up of 3 things: 
- The effect of the curse
- The level of the curse
- The way to break it.

### Diseases
Caught from filth-covered arrows, verminous bites, or the breath of the damned—diseases fester beneath the skin, sapping Strength and spirit alike. A Disease is build up of 5 things: 
- The Stage of the Disease
- The effect of the different Stages
- The means by which the disease is contracted
- The level of the Disease
- The way to cure it.

### Impairments
Scars of birth, wounds of war, or the creeping toll of age—impairments are the quiet curses that linger without a spell. An Impairment is build up of 3 things:
- The effect of the Impairment
- The level of the Impairment
- The operation needed to mend it.
