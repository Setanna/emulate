<h1> Bestiary <span style="margin-left: auto;">1 SC</span> </h1>

[[Book]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

___
*A book containing monsters of all shape and sizes.*

<br>

**System:**
When you use the [[Scour the Pages]] action with this tome, you gain a +2 [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Item|Item]] Bonus to the [[Monster Lore]] Action until the end of your turn.

<br>

Additionally if you Critically Succeed you can choose one of the following bonuses against all creatures of the same race until the end of the encounter:
- You gain a +2 [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Proficiency|Proficiency]] Bonus when rolling damage against the creature.
- You gain a +2 [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Proficiency|Proficiency]]  Bonus to your Hit Class and Saving Throws against the creature.
