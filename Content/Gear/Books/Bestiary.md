*A book containing monsters of all shape and sizes.*

<br>

**System:**
When you use the [[Scour the Pages]] action with this tome, you gain a +2 Knowledge Bonus to your Knowledge roll to identify a creature. 

<br>

Additionally if you Critically Succeed you can choose one of the following bonuses against all creatures of the same type until the end of the encounter:
- You gain a +1 Knowledge Bonus when rolling damage against the creature.
- You gain a +1 Knowledge Bonus to your Hit Class against the creature.
