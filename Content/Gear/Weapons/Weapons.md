# Weapons

### Melee Weapons
Melee Weapons are Strength Based and as such add Strength to Attack Rolls and Damage Rolls.

<br>

With a Melee Weapon you can Attack anyone within your Reach and [[Line of Sight]].

### Ranged Weapons
Ranged Weapons are Dexterity Based and as such add Dexterity to Attack Rolls and to Damage Rolls.

<br>

With a Ranged Weapon you can Attack anyone within Maximum Range Increment and [[Line of Sight]]. A Ranged Weapon's Maximum Range is equal to 5 times their Range Increment. For each Increment after the first the Attack Roll gets a -2 [[Circumstance]] Penalty.
