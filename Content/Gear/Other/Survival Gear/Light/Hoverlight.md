<h1> Hoverlight <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 10 cc </span> </h1>

<div style="clear:both" /> 

___
*A smooth pale stone, once activated shines like a small sun.*

<br>

**System:**
A Hoverlight can be activated using the [[Interact]] action. Once Activated the pebble will float above the users head and shed [[Lighting | Norma Lighting]] for 6 meters and [[Lighting | Dim Light]] for another 6 meters. 

<br>

The Hoverlight can be activated for up to 24 Hours before turning into a regular pebble.
