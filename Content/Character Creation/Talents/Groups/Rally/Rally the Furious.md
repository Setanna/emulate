<h1> Rally the Furious <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 2 XP</span> </h1>

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** [[Content/Actions/Talent Actions/Rally | Rally]]
___
*Some lead with wisdom and age. You lead with fire and steel*

<br>

**System:**
When you take the [[Content/Actions/Talent Actions/Rally | Rally]] Action it gains the following option:

##### My Soldiers Rage!
Your allies gain the [[Content/Actions/Talent Actions/Rage | Rage]] Action and can [[Content/Actions/Talent Actions/Rage | Rage]] as a Free Action without meeting the requirements of [[Content/Actions/Talent Actions/Rage | Rage]], when affected by this [[Content/Actions/Talent Actions/Rally | Rally]].
If they lose the benefits of this [[Content/Actions/Talent Actions/Rally | Rally]], they also loses the benefits of the [[Content/Actions/Talent Actions/Rage | Rage]] Action,
if the ally already have the [[Content/Actions/Talent Actions/Rage | Rage]] Action they only lose the benefits of the [[Content/Actions/Talent Actions/Rage | Rage]] Action if they do not meet it's requirements.
