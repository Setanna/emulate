<h1> Spellbane <span style="margin-left: auto;">6 XP</span> </h1>

[[Defence]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** Constitution 3+, No Talents with the [[Arcane]] Trait.
___
*Your very heart beats in defiance of the arcane.*

<br>

**System:**
You can no longer take or gain Talents with the [[Arcane]] trait, however you gain the following bonuses against Sources with the [[Arcane]] Trait:
- You gain a +1 [[Resistance]] Bonus to [[saving throws]].
- You can use your Constitution instead of Dexterity or Wisdom for [[saving throws]].
- Weapon used against you loose the Arcane Trait and all benefits gained from it.
- If you would take damage, reduce an amount of damage equal to your Constitution (Weapons no longer possess the [[Arcane]] Trait against you)
