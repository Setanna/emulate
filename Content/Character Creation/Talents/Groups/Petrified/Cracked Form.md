<h1> Cracked Form <span style="margin-left: auto;">1 XP</span> </h1>

[[Defence]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** Dexterity 1+
___
*Even stone must crack to let the wind pass through.*

<br>

**System:**
Whenever you would take a [[Circumstance]] Penalty Die from the [[Petrified]] Condition on your Dexterity Based Attacks, Skills or Saving Throws, reduce the die by one step. You reduce the die by one additional step for each talent you have with Cracked Form as a requirement.

<br>

Additionally you reduce the Armor Value gained from [[Petrified]] by one step.
