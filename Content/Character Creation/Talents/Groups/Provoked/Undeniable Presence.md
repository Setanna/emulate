<h1> Undeniable Presence <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 4 XP</span> </h1>

[[Sight]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** [[Between All Eyes]] or [[Grand Reveal]], Charisma 3+
___
*In the chaos of war, you're the one they can't ignore.*

<br>

**System:**
Any creature that is [[Provoked]] with you as their Target, can not reduce their [[Provoked]] Value below your Charisma, for as long as they have sight to you. However the [[Provoked]] Condition can still start at a lower value than your Charisma.

<br>

Additionally, you can use the [[All Eyes On Me]] and [[Dramatic Reveal]] Talents even when you are [[Surprised]].
