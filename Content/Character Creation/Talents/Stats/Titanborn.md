<h1> Titanborn <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 3 XP</span> </h1>

[[Birthright]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** Strength 3+
___
*Your strength knows no bounds, allowing you to perform feats beyond mortal limits.*

<br>

**System:**
You count as a size larger for [[Stats#Strength | Carrying Capacity]] and the [[Leap]] Action. Additionally your speed increases by 1 meter and when taking the [[Step]] Action you can move an additional meter.

<br>

Your height and weight are the maximum for your race as written in their description.
