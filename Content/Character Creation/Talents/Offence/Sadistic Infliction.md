**System:**
Whenever you deal at least a single point of damage, you gain a +1 [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Morale | Morale]] on attack, damage and Skill rolls until the end of your next turn. If you kill a creature increase this bonus to +3 on the next attack or Skill roll, if the attack or Skill would deal damage increase the damage bonus to +3.
