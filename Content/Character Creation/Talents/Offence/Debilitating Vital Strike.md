<h1> Debilitating Vital Strike <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 2 XP</span> </h1>

[[Flexible]]{.trait}

[[Offence]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** Strength 1
___

**System:**
When you Attack with a Weapon that has the [[Vital Strike]] Trait, you can reduce the Bleed Damage Die by one step to give the creature one of the following effects until the creature regains any Hit Points:
 - **Go for the tendons:** The creature's Speed is reduced by 1
 - **Go for the Throat:** The [[Damage Types | Bleed]] Damage is applied for 2 rounds instead of just 1.
 - **Go for the Muscle:** The Creature gains a -2 [[Circumstance]] Penalty to Attack Rolls.
