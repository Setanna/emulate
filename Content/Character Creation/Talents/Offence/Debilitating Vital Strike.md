<h1> Debilitating Vital Strike <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 4 XP</span> </h1>

[[Finesse]]{.trait}

[[Offence]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** Strength 1+
___

**System:**
When you deal damage with a Weapon that has the [[Vital Strike]] Trait, you can reduce the Bleed Damage Die by one step to give the creature one of the following effects for as long as they have the [[Bleeding]] Condition:
 - **Go for the Tendons:** The creature's Speed is reduced by one
 - **Go for the Throat:** The [[Bleeding]] Condition gains the [[Lasting]] Trait.
 - **Go for the Muscle:** The Creature gains a -1 [[Circumstance]] Penalty to Attack Rolls.
