<h1> Advanced Weapon Proficiency <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 9 XP </span> </h1>

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** [[Simple Weapon Proficiency]]
___

**System:**
You become [[Rules/Stats and Skills/Proficiency | Proficient]] in Advanced Weapons.

<br>

Additionally, whenever you make an [[Rules/Traits/Attack | Attack]] Action, you can unsheathe an Advanced Weapon on your person as part of the Action.
