#### [[Defence]]{.trait}

</div> 
<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** Constitution 2+
___
*A latent resistance within you blunts the edge of hostile magic.*

<br>

**System:**
You gain a +1 Morale Bonus to attack and damage rolls with weapons as long as you have less than your Maximum Hit Points. The Morale Bonus increases to +2 whenever you have less than half Hit Points. When you're at Negative Hit Points the Morale Bonus increases to +3.
