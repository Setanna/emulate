<h1> Runic Study <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 8 XP</span> </h1>

[[Arcane]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** Intelligence 2+, Basic Runic
___
*Runes of creation and destruction, humming with ancient secrets*

<br>

**System:**
You trace runes in the air using magical instruments to summon powerful effects. You gain a number of Spell Points equal to your Intelligence and regain any expended Spell Points at the end of a [[Resting | Long Rest]].

<br>

Additionally you learn 5 Spells of your choice, you do not need to meet the Spell Requirements of any spell you choose.
