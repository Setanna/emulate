<h1> Rally the Fallen <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 1 XP</span> </h1>

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** [[Rally]]
___
*Your words rally the fallen to their feet and lets them face death with a sword in their hand.*

<br>

**System:**
When you take the [[Rally]] Action, any ally who is [[Deafened]] due to being [[Unconscious]] is still affected by your [[Rally]] Action. Additionally your [[Rally]] Action gains the following option:

##### Last Stand
Your allies gain the [[Indomitable]] Talent. Allies who already have the Talent instead add your Charisma as a [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Morale|Morale]] Bonus to their Negative Hit Points.

<br>

Additionally, any ally who is [[Unconscious]] due to being at Negative Hit Points when they gain the [[Indomitable]] Talent, lose the [[Unconscious]] Condition. If they have the [[Unconscious]] Condition for any other reason, it is not removed.
