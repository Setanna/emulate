*With a slash, the wind becomes your weapon, tearing through anything in its path.*

<br>

**System:**
All your Melee Weapons that deal Slashing Damage gain the [[Rules/Traits/Reach|Reach]] Trait with a value equal to your Strength. If you would make an Attack Roll outside your original  [[Rules/Traits/Reach|Reach]], the Attack Roll takes a -3 [[Circumstance]] Penalty.
