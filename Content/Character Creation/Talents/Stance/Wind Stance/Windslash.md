*With a slash, the wind becomes your weapon, tearing through anything in its path.*

<br>

**System:**
Whenever you would wield a Melee Slashing Weapon, it gains the [[Rules/Traits/Reach | Reach]] Trait with a value equal to your Strength.

<br>

When you make a [[Making a Strike | Strike]] or [[Making a Sweep | Sweep]] against a creature that is outside your normal melee reach and can only be targeted due to this [[Rules/Traits/Reach | Reach]] trait, the Attack takes a -3 [[Circumstance]] Penalty.
