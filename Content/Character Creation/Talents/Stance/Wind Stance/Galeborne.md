*Air itself bears your weight*

**System:**
When you are [[Falling]], reduce the damage taken by your Dexterity squared *(Dexterity x Dexterity)*. If you would take no damage from [[Falling]], you don't get the [[Prone | Prone 3]] Condition. *[[Stats#Strength | Medium and Heavy Loads]] come with Dexterity penalties.*

Additionally, when you would take the [[Leap]] Action, add your Dexterity to your Strength for any calculations made regarding said Action.
