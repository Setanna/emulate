**Effect:**
The target's heart is filled with pure light. The target gains a the [[Lumination | Lumination (10 meters)]] Trait The target gains a [[Resistance]] Bonus to their [[Saving Throws | Fortitude]] Saving Throw equal to the caster's Spell Stat.

<br>

Additionally, the target may Roll a [[Saving Throws | Recovery (Fortitude)]] Saving Throw.

<br>

**System:**
For as long as this spell is active you gain the following Action:
 - [[Luminescent Detonation | Luminescent Detonation (Heart of Purity)]]
