*Bone twists, flesh reshapes—your hands rewrite death itself..*

<br>

**System:**
You mold a corpse within reach, choose one of the following effects:
- **Restore:** You restore any damage or decay to the corpse, the corpse regains 1d4 + Spell Stat Hit Points. When it reaches the Maximum Hit Points it has been returned to pristine condition.
- **Decay:** You decay the corpse down to its bones, if there is only bones left you can turn it to dust.
- **Alter:** You mold the flesh of the corpse, removing or adding characteristics, such as a sharp jaw, a mole on the upper lip or even the height or weight of the corpse. The weight or height can't be greater or less than their described height and weight.
