#  Spells

## Traits
All spells automatically gain the [[Arcane]] and [[Focus]] Traits.

## Attributes
A Spell is made up of 6 attributes: 

### Level
The level of a spell determines how many Spell Points is needed to cast it. Some spells have a Level of 0 meaning they cost nothing to cast, but they can be uppercasted, meaning a Spell Point can be spend to increase the effect of the spell.

### Actions
How many actions it takes to cast a spell.

### Area
The Area of a spell can be any of the following:
- **Single Target:** A single target within range
- **Multi Target:** A number of targets within range
- **Cone:** A cone of varied length
- **Sphere:** A cone of varied radius
- **Line:** A line of varied length

### Duration
The duration of a spell can be any of the following:
- **Instantaneous:** The spells effect resolve immediately
- **Encounter:** The spell lasts until the end of the encounter
- **Preparation:** The spell lasts until the next preparation

### Range
A spell can have 
- **Personal:** The area originates from the caster
- **Touch:** The area targets within reach.
- **Short Range:** 5 meters + 1 per Spell Point 
- **Medium Range:** 10 meters + 2 per Spell Point
- **Long Range:** 20 meters + 4 per Spell Point

### Damage
A Spell deals damage in form of Damage Dice, similar to weapons. Some Spells deal additional damage based on your Spell Stat. Your Spell Stat is the Stat used to Cast the Spell as defined by the Talent that granted you the Spellcasting.

### School
The school of a spell typically defines what the spell is able to do.
