<h1> They All Have Names <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 2 XP</span> </h1>

[[Bonded]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" />

**Requirements:** Charisma 1+
___
*"That’s Gravyfoot, this is Saddle Mike, and don’t get near Toothwitch”*
*— Nettie Fenn, Friend of Fur and Feather*

<br>

**System:**
Whenever you or your group, would get a new [[Animal]], you can name it. 
The [[Animal]] gains a +1 [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Morale | Morale]] Bonus to their Wisdom. Additionally it will react when it's name is called, but not necessarily do anything more.
All [[Animal | Animals]] you name with this Social Bond, are considered your bonded creatures.
