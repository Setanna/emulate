___
*A glance is worth a thousand words—especially when you're in on the joke.*

<br>

**System:**
When you take this Social Bond, choose any number of Characters. You and those characters can communicate basic ideas through minor facial movements such as nodding or raising an eyebrow.

<br>

The basic ideas can not be more advanced than "Let us Attack the child first"; "I'm going to steal this, distract them"; "He looks like he has loot" or "Run for it".
