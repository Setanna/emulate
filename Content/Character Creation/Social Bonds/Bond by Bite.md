<h1> Bond by Bite <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 4 XP</span> </h1>

<div style="clear:both" />

**Requirements:** [[They All Have Names]], Charisma 2+
___
*"Apples for the donkey. Liver scraps for the fox. Don’t mix ‘em up”*
*— Nettie Fenn, Friend of Fur and Feather*

<br>

**System:**
You learn the favorite food of all [[Animal | Animals]] you have named with [[They All Have Names]], as chosen by the GM. Each favorite food must cost between 10 cc and 1 sp, not accounting for size of the animal.

<br>
 
At each [[Resting | Preparation]] you can feed your [[Bonded]] [[Animal | Animals]] their favorite food.
For each [[Bonded]] [[Animal | Animal]] you feed their favorite food they gain one of the following benefits (Chosen by you):
 - The [[Animal | Animal's]] Speed increases by two.
 - The [[Animal | Animal's]] Wisdom increases by one.
 - One of the [[Animal | Animal's]] Physical Stat increases by one, to a maximum of 5.
