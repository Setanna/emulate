# Afflictions
Afflictions can vary from curses to a limp. Any character can gain an Affliction during their escapades, but some can start with them from birth.

## Starting with an Affliction.
To start with an Affliction, simply apply the Affliction the the character, and increase the XP of said Character by the level of the Affliction.

### Curses
Evil Witches or wishes gone wrong—a curse can be the result of many things, but always stems from magic. A Curse is build up of 3 things: The effect of the curse; the level of the curse; and the way to break it.

### Diseases
Caught from filth-covered arrows, verminous bites, or the breath of the damned—diseases fester beneath the skin, sapping Strength and spirit alike. A Disease is build up of 4 things: The Stage of the Disease; the effect of the different Stages; the level of the Disease; and the way to cure it.

### Impairments
Scars of birth, wounds of war, or the creeping toll of age—impairments are the quiet curses that linger without a spell. An Impairment is build up of 3 things: The effect of the Impairment; the level of the Impairment; and the operation needed to mend it.
