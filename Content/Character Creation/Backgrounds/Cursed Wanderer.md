<h1> Cursed Wanderer <span style="margin-left: auto;">- XP</span> </h1>

<div style="clear:both" /> 

___
*A dark force lingers over you—perhaps a family curse or a tainted wish.*

<br>

**System:**
You start with a Curse. You can either roll randomly on the table below or choose one of the curses.


|  #  | XP  | Curse          | Effect                                                                                       |
|:---:|:---:| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  | -6  | Unlucky        | You gain a -2 [[Luck]] Penalty on your hit class; difficulty checks; and rolls.              |
|  2  | -3  | Grotesque      | You gain the [[Ugly]] and [[Hideous]] Trait.                                                 |
|  3  | -2  | Haunted Dreams | You need 10 Hours for a [[Resting \| Long Rest]].                                            |
|  4  | -8  | Withering Body | Whenever you would regain Hit Points you regain 2 less to a minimum of 1 Hit Point regained. |
|  5  | -10 | Oathbound      | If you ever break a promise, as determined by the GM, you permanently loose 1 Hit Points.    |
|  6  | -6  | Hollow Heart   | You gain the [[Emotionless]] Trait.                                                          | 

The DC to dispel the curse is 25 + the amount of XP gained from the curse. For Example the Hollow Heart DC would be 31.
