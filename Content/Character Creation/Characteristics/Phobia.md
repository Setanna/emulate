**System:**
Choose a specific, narrow category of creature or thing (e.g. spiders, scaled creatures, corpses, deep water). If the Game Master allows your choice, it is now considered your Phobia.

<br>

Whenever you [[Perception | Perceive]] your Phobia, depending on the severity of the situation you gain the Frightened Condition with the Persistent Trait.

<br>

> [!info] Example
> This example is based on Nettie Fenn, who has a Phobia of "Scaled Creatures".
> 
> | Severity | Value | Example                                |
> | -------- | :---: | --------------------------------------- |
> | Low      |   1   | A snake or lizard within arm's reach |
> | Minor    |   2   | A nest of snakes or a large crocodile   |
> | Major    |   3   | A giant serpent or a pit full of snakes |
> | Critical |   4   | A dragon or horde of small lizards     |
