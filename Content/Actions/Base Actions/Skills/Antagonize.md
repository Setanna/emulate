___

**System:**
You can use [[Diplomacy]], [[Bluff]] or [[Intimidation]] to make a creature target their hatred towards you or another creature you can see. Make the Chosen Skill Roll against all Enemies Will DC. If the creature is told to target their own ally they gain a +5 [[Circumstance]] Bonus to their Will DC.


<br>

**Critical Success:** The creature gains the [[Provoked | Provoked 2]] Condition.
**Success:** The creature gains the [[Provoked | Provoked 1]] Condition.
**Fail:** You fail to provoke the enemy.
**Critical Fail:** The creatures indifference provokes you. You gain the [[Provoked | Provoked 1]] Condition, with the creature as your Target.
