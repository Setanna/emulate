<h1> Swift Grip <span style="margin-left: auto;"> Reaction </span> </h1>

[[Skill | Skill (Climb)]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" />

**Requirements:** Both hands free.
**Trigger:** You are [[Falling]].
___

**System:**
You Roll a Climb Roll against the DC of the surface you are falling past. While holding onto the edge you are [[Flat-Footed]].

<br>

Alternatively you can use only one hand to grab onto an edge, leaving the other hand free for a torch or weapon, doing so incurs a -4 [[Circumstance]] Penalty to the Climb Roll.

<br>

**Critical Success:** You grab onto an edge without taking damage.
**Success:** You take bludgeoning damage equal to half the meters fallen rounded down.,
**Fail:** You fail to grab onto an edge.
