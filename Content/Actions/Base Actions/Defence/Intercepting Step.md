<h1> Intercepting Step <span style="margin-left: auto;"> Reaction </span> </h1>

[[Defence]]{.trait}

 
<div style="clear:both" />

**Trigger:** An ally is the target of a Charge Action; Ranged Attack; or Makes a Saving Throw from a source with the [[Blast]] Trait.
___

**System:**
You take the [[Step]] Action to place yourself between the attack and the ally, or move closer to the ally.
