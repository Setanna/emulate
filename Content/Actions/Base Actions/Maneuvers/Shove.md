<h1> Shove <span style="margin-left: auto;"> Seconds </span> </h1>

[[Maneuver]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

___

**System:**
You Roll Athletics against target creatures [[Athletics]] or [[Acrobatics]] DC, whichever is higher. You move with the creature you shove, up to your speed.

<br>

**Critical Success:** You move the creature up to your Strength in meters and it gains the [[Prone]] Condition.
**Success:** You move the creature 1 meter
**Failure:** Nothing happens
**Critical Fail:** You gain the [[Prone]] Condition.
