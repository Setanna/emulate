<h1> Rage<span style="margin-left: auto;"> 1 Action</span> </h1>

[[Morale]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" />

**Requirements:** You have less than or equal to half your Maximum Hit Points Left
___
*Fury consumes you, unlocking unparalleled Strength and a relentless will to fight*

<br>

**System:**
You gain the following bonuses and penalties until the End of the Encounter or until you use Rage again:
- +2 [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Morale|Morale]] Bonus to your Strength
- -2 [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Morale|Morale]] Penalty on Hit Class.
- You gain a number of [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to your currently missing Hit Points
- You gain the [[Brave]] Characteristic. If you already have the Characteristic, instead double the bonus it grants.
