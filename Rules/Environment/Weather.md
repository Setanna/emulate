## Fog
Fog can vary from a light gray covering the landside to a thick mist that swallows the landscape, obscuring all but the closest shapes in a ghostly haze.

<br>

There are three types of fog; Thin, Dense and Thick as shown below:

<br>

**Thin Fog:**
Thin Fog is barely noticeable. The Perception Modifier for the Distance to Source Sight Modifier is doubled to +2 per 10 meters.

**Dense Fog:**
Dense Fog can cover large areas and obscure details or even object from a distance. The Perception Modifier for the Distance to Source Sight Modifier is doubled from Thin Fog to +4 per 10 meters.

## Rain
Rain can vary from a light drizzle to a tsunami from the heavens.

| Rain                     | Wet \| Wet Condition          | Perception | Survival |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------- | ---------- | -------- |
| Clear Skies or a Drizzle | -                             | 0          | 0        |
| Light Rain               | [[Drenched\| Drenched (1d2)]] | -4         | -4       |
| Moderate Rain            | [[Drenched\| Drenched (1d4)]] | -6         | -6       |
| Heavy Rain               | [[Drenched\| Drenched (1d6)]] | -8         | -8       |
	
#### Wet Condition
After a creature enters an area of Rain or starts their turn in rain, it gains the Condition as shown in the table above.

#### Perception
Creatures take a [[Circumstance]] Penalty on all Sight, Vision and Scent Based Perception Rolls against sources in Rain or behind Rain.

#### Survival
Creatures take a [[Circumstance]] Penalty on all Survival Rolls to Track creatures. This Penalty is applied to the creatures tracks each hour, until it reaches -24 Penalty from the rain, after which the tracks are completely washed away.
