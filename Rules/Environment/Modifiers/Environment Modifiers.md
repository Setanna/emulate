# Environment Modifiers
Environment Modifiers can be added to a wide and varied things such as Fog or just Woods. Modifiers simply add another interesting element to the Battlefield.

<br>

Each Modifier has a Level based on the effect and any Difficulty Check for the Modifier. 

<br>

If all enemies in [[Encounters#Combat Encounters | Combat Encounters]] have immunity or otherwise ignore the Environment Modifier, you should increase their XP Cost increased by 5 times the Modifier's Level.
