# Natural Attacks

> [!warning] Work In Progress
> This has not been properly play tested
> This is written as RAI and will be polished at a later time

Natural Attacks are tied to the physical attributes of a Creature. These attacks are defined by both their **form** (such as Claws or Bite) and their **power** (Damage Die).

---

## Damage & Cost

| Damage Die | XP  | Possible Traits                                   |
| ---------- | --- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 1d4        | 4   | [[Versatile \| Versatile (b, p or s)]], [[Light]] |
| 1d6        | 8   | [[Versatile \| Versatile (b, p or s)]], [[Light]] |
| 1d8        | 16  | [[Versatile \| Versatile (b, p or s)]]            |
| 1d10       | 32  | [[Versatile \| Versatile (b, p or s)]], [[Heavy]] |
| 1d12       | 64  | [[Versatile \| Versatile (b, p or s)]], [[Heavy]] |

All Natural Attacks have the [[Natural]] Trait.

Additional Traits may be purchased for **8 XP each**.

The Damage Type of a Natural Attack must be one of the [[Damage Types | Physical Damage]] types unless otherwise stated.

---

## Natural Attack Forms

Natural Attacks are grouped into different **forms**, representing how the attack is made. Each form determines the default damage type and flavor of the attack.

---

### Bite

> **Bite** *(Natural Attack)*  
> You attack using your teeth or jaws.  
>
> - Default Damage Type: **Piercing** or **Slashing**  
> - You may use this attack even when your hands are occupied.

---

### Claws

> **Claws** *(Natural Attack)*  
> You possess one or more clawed limbs.
>
> - Default Damage Type: **Slashing**
>
> When you gain this Natural Attack, choose any number of appropriate limbs (such as hands or similar appendages). Those limbs are considered clawed.
>
> Regardless of how many clawed limbs you have, this counts as a single Natural Attack unless an ability states otherwise.

---

### Tail

> **Tail** *(Natural Attack)*  
> You attack using a powerful tail.  
>
> - Default Damage Type: **Bludgeoning**  
> - May be used to strike targets behind or beside you, depending on GM discretion.

---

### Horns

> **Horns** *(Natural Attack)*  
> You attack using horns or similar protrusions.  
>
> - Default Damage Type: **Piercing**  
> - Typically used for forward or charging attacks.

---

### Wings

> **Wings** *(Natural Attack)*  
> You attack using your wings.  
>
> - Default Damage Type: **Bludgeoning**  
> - Wings may also be used for non-combat purposes (such as flight) if granted by another feature.

---

## Limb-Based Attacks

Some Natural Attacks are tied to specific limbs (such as Claws or Wings).

- You may assign these attacks to any appropriate limbs your character possesses.
- Having multiple such limbs does not grant additional attacks.
- Additional attacks must be granted by separate abilities.

---

## Creating a Natural Attack

To create a Natural Attack:

1. Choose a **Form** (Bite, Claws, Tail, etc.)
2. Choose a **Damage Die** from the table above
3. Select a **Damage Type** (if applicable)
4. Purchase any additional **Traits**

---

## Notes

- Some Natural Attacks represent **Anatomical Features** (such as Tail or Horns).  
  These may exist without being used as attacks unless upgraded or modified by other abilities.

- Multiple Natural Attacks may be possessed, but each must be purchased separately unless stated otherwise.
