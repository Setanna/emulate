# Size
Size modifies several things regarding a creature as noted in the table below:

| Size       | Height (m)  | Weight (Kg)  | Modifier | Move | Strength Rolls | Dexterity Rolls | Attack | Hit Class | Weapon Damage | Damage Reduction |
| ---------- | ----------- | ------------ |:--------:|:----:|:--------------:|:---------------:|:------:|:---------:|:-------------:|:----------------:|
| Diminutive | 0.1 to 0.3  | 0.25 to 0.5  |    -8    |  -8  |       -8       |       +8        |   +8   |    +8     |      -8       |        -8        |
| Tiny       | 0.3 to 0.6  | 0.5 to 4     |    -4    |  -4  |       -4       |       +4        |   +4   |    +4     |      -4       |        -4        |
| Small      | 0.6 to 1.2  | 4 to 32      |    -2    |  -2  |       -2       |       +2        |   +2   |    +2     |      -2       |        -2        |
| Medium     | 1.2 to 2.4  | 32 to 256    |    0     |  0   |       0        |        0        |   0    |     0     |       0       |        0         |
| Large      | 2.4 to 4.8  | 256 to 2048  |    +2    |  +2  |       +2       |       -2        |   -2   |    -2     |      +2       |        +2        |
| Huge       | 4.8 to 9.6  | 2048 to 4096 |    +4    |  +4  |       +4       |       -4        |   -4   |    -4     |      +4       |        +4        |
| Gargantuan | 9.6 to 19.2 | 4096 to 8192 |    +8    |  +8  |       +8       |       -8        |   -8   |    -8     |      +8       |        +8        |

<br>

**Move**
Whenever you would forcefully move a creature, such as with the [[Ram]] or [[Shove]] Actions, increase the meters the creature is moved by your Move Modifier. 

<br>

Whenever you would be forcefully moved by a creature, such a with the [[Ram]] or [[Shove]] Actions, decrease the meters moved by your Move Modifier.

<br>

**Strength Rolls**
Whenever you would make a Strength-Based Skill Roll, add your Strength Roll Modifier as a [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Size | Size]] Bonus or Penalty.

For creatures larger than medium, double the cost and weight of armor, food, shields and weapons for each size larger. For smaller creatures half the cost and weight of armor, food, shields and weapons for each size smaller.
