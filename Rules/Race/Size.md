# Size
Size modifies several things regarding a creature as noted in the table below:

| Size       | Height (m)  | Weight (Kg)  | Flight | Stealth | Attack | Hit Class | Weapon Damage | Damage Reduction |
| ---------- | ----------- | ------------ |:------:|:-------:|:------:|:---------:|:-------------:|:----------------:|
| Diminutive | 0.1 to 0.3  | 0.25 to 0.5  |   +8   |   +8    |   +8   |    +8     |      -8       |        -8        |
| Tiny       | 0.3 to 0.6  | 0.5 to 4     |   +4   |   +4    |   +4   |    +4     |      -4       |        -4        |
| Small      | 0.6 to 1.2  | 4 to 32      |   +2   |   +2    |   +2   |    +2     |      -2       |        -2        |
| Medium     | 1.2 to 2.4  | 32 to 256    |   0    |    0    |   0    |     0     |       0       |        0         |
| Large      | 2.4 to 4.8  | 256 to 2048  |   -2   |   -2    |   -2   |    -2     |      +2       |        +2        |
| Huge       | 4.8 to 9.6  | 2048 to 4096 |   -4   |   -4    |   -4   |    -4     |      +4       |        +4        |
| Gargantuan | 9.6 to 19.2 | 4096 to 8192 |   -8   |   -8    |   -8   |    -8     |      +8       |        +8        | 

<br>

For creatures larger than medium, double the cost and weight of armor, food, shields and weapons for each size larger. For smaller creatures half the cost and weight of armor, food, shields and weapons for each size smaller.
