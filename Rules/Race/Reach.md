# Reach
Reach determines how long a creature can reach for with [[Interact]] Actions; and Actions with the [[Rules/Traits/Attack | Attack]] Trait.

<br>

A Creature's Reach is most of the time dependant on their size and weapon they're using.


| Size   | Reach                         |
| ------ | ----------------------------- |
| Tiny   | 0 Meters (Same Square or Hex) |
| Small  | 1 Meter                       |
| Medium | 1 Meter                       |
| Large  | 1 Meters                      | 
| Huge   | 2 Meters                      |
