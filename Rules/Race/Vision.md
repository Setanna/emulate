# Vision

> [!warning] Work In Progress
> Vision will be overhauled in the future

Races of Emulate can come with a multitude of different ways to see the world. The 3 most common being: Regular Vision, Low-Light Vision and Dark-Vision.

### Regular Vision
Creatures with Regular Vision gain penalties and bonuses as per [[Lighting]].

### Low-Light Vision
Creatures with Low-Light Vision function like Regular Vision, but they treat [[Lighting#Dim Light | Dim Light]] as [[Lighting#Lighting | Normal Light]].

### Dark Vision
Creatures with Dark-Vision function like Regular Vision, but they treat [[Lighting#Dim Light | Dim Light]] and [[Lighting#Complete Darkness | Complete Darkness]] as [[Lighting#Lighting | Normal Light]].

### Light Sensitivity
Creatures with Light Sensitivity treat [[Lighting#Bright Light | Bright Light]] as [[Lighting#Dim Light | Dim Light]] regarding penalties.
