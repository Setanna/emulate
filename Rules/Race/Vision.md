# Vision
There are 3 types of vision a creature can possess: Regular Vision, Low-Light Vision and Dark-Vision.

<br>

### Regular Vision
Creatures with Regular Vision gain penalties and bonuses as per [[Lighting]].

### Low-Light Vision
Creatures with Low-Light Vision treat [[Lighting#Dim Light | Dim Light]] as Normal Light.

### Dark Vision
Creatures with Dark-Vision treat [[Lighting#Dim Light | Dim Light]] and [[Lighting#Complete Darkness | Complete Darkness]] as Normal Light.

### Light Sensitivity
Creatures with Light Sensitivity treat [[Lighting#Bright Light | Bright Light]] as [[Lighting#Dim Light | Dim Light]] regarding penalties.
