# Natural Armor
Some creatures are born with thick skin that can soften blows and deflect arrows, the opposite is however also true, creatures so fragile that a nonlethal blow would land them in the afterlife. A creature with Natural Armor increases their Armor Value by an amount equal to their Natural Armor Value.


| Natural Armor | XP  |
| ------------- | --- |
| -2            | -4  |
| -1            | -2  |
| 0             | 0   |
| +1            | +2  |
| +2            | +4  |
