# Natural Attack
Some creatures are born with claws and teeth made to shred their prey.


| Damage Die | XP  | Possible Traits |
| ---------- | --- | --------------- |
| 1d4        | 2   | [[Versatile \| Versatile (b, p or s)]], [[Light]]       |
| 1d6        | 4   | [[Versatile \| Versatile (b, p or s)]], [[Light]]       |
| 1d8        | 6   | [[Versatile \| Versatile (b, p or s)]],                 |
| 1d10       | 8   | [[Versatile \| Versatile (b, p or s)]], [[Heavy]]       |
| 1d12       | 10  | [[Versatile \| Versatile (b, p or s)]], [[Heavy]]       | 

All Natural Attacks have the [[Natural]] Trait, and additional Traits can be purchased for 2 XP Each. The Damage Type of the Natural Attack is one of the [[Damage Types | Physical Damage]] Types.
