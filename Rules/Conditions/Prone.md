# Prone
You take a [[Circumstance]] Penalty on your Hit Class; Attack Rolls and Reflex Saving Throws, the Penalty is equal to the value of this Condition. Additionally the only [[Movement]] Actions you can take are [[Stand]] or [[Crawl]].

<br>

If you would gain the Prone Condition, while you're Climbing or Flying, you fall. You can't gain the Prone Condition when Swimming.

<br>

If you are sitting, kneeling or laying down or other similar actions, you automatically gain the Prone Condition as shown in the table below:

| Value        | Example                                |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Prone 1      | Kneeling or crouching                  |
| Prone 2      | Sitting on a chair or squatting        |
| Prone 3      | Laying down or sitting on the ground   |

<br>

You can never gain a higher Prone value than three.
