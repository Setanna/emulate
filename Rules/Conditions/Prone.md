# Prone
You are lying on the ground or sitting down. You take a -2 [[Circumstance]] Penalty on your Attack Rolls and Hit Class while you are Prone. Additionally the only [[Movement]] Actions you can take are [[Stand]] or [[Crawl]].

If you would gain the Prone Condition, while you're Climbing or Flying, you fall. You can't gain the Prone Condition when Swimming.
