# Freezing
A creature that is Freezing rolls their Petrified Die as a [[Circumstance]] Penalty on all Attack Rolls, Physical Skills and Reflex Saving Throws. And for each step of the Freezing Die, the creature reduces their speed by that amount. So a d4 would reduce a creatures speed by 1 and a d10 would reduce a creatures speed by 4. 

<br>

When a creatures reaches a d12 Freezing Value, they and any items on their person, freeze completely. The creature gains the [[Unconscious]] Condition until the Freezing Value is reduced below a d12.
