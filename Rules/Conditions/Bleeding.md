# Bleeding
At the start of a Bleeding Creatures turn they take damage equal to the Damage Die in the parenthesis of the condition, then reduce the Damage Die by one step. 

<br>

This damage has no damage type and creatures without blood cant be affected by this Trait. Additionally if a creature would regain any number of Hit Points, this condition is removed.
