# Blinded
A creature that is blind gains the [[Flat-Footed]] condition and all Actions they take loose the [[Accurate]] Trait. Additionally they automatically fail any [[Perception | Perception (Sight)]] rolls. Blind creatures concur a -8 Circumstance Penalty to Strength and Dexterity Based rolls.

<br>

Additionally a blinded creature is immune from anything with the [[Sight]] Trait.

<br>

A Blinded creature must use their hearing or another sense to determine the location of creatures.
