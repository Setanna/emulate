**System:**
For as long as you have the Blinded Condition you have the following effects:
- You gain the [[Flat-Footed]] Condition
- All Actions you take loose the [[Accurate]] Trait.
- You automatically fail any [[Perception | Perception (Sight)]] Skill Checks
- You gain [[Immunity | Immunity (Sight)]]
- You take a -8 [[Circumstance]] Penalty to your
	- Attack Rolls
	- Reflex Saving Throws
	- Strength and Dexterity Based Skill Checks.

*A Blinded creature must use their hearing or another sense to determine the location of creatures*
