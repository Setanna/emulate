# Frightened

[[Fleeting]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

___
*You hesitate, even when you shouldn’t*

**System:**
You take a [[Morale]] Penalty on your Attack Rolls, Saving Throws, and Skill Checks. The Penalty is equal to your Frightened Condition's Value.
