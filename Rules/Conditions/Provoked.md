# Provoked
A creature that has the Provoked Condition, takes a [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Morale | Morale]] Penalty on all rolls against other creatures and all other creatures gain a [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Morale | Morale]] Bonus against effects from the Provoked creature. The Morale Bonus or Penalty is equal to the creature's Provoked value.

<br>

The Provoked Creature is tunnel visioned onto one creature, referred to as their target. Their target does not gain any [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Morale | Morale]] Bonus on affects from the Provoked Creature, nor does the Provoked Creature take a [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Morale | Morale]] Penalty on rolls against their Target.

<br>

At the end of the creature's next turn, and each turn after, the Provoked Condition is reduced by one.
