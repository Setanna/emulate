# Making a Strike
A Creature making a Strike is considered the attacker. While the target of the Strike is considered the defender.

### 1. Choose Weapon
The attacker chooses what weapon they are wielding to Strike with. They can alternatively choose to use [[Unarmed Strikes]].

### 2. Determine Targets
The attacker chooses a creature within their reach (for melee attacks) or range (for ranged attacks) that they have [[Line of Sight]] to.

### 3. Roll Damage
The attacker Rolls the Weapon's Damage Die and adds their Strength for melee attacks or their Dexterity for ranged attacks. 

*Damage Rolled can never be less than 1.*

##### 3.1 Apply Damage Modifiers
Apply any and all modifier to the Damage Roll using the [[Order of Operations]].

### 4. Determine Success
The attacker makes an Attack Roll against the defender's [[Saving Throws#Reflex | Reflex]] DC.

**Critical Success:** The defender takes the maximum damage the attack could deal (As shown in step 4.1)
**Success:** The defender takes full damage
**Fail:** The defender takes no damage
**Critical Fail:** The defender takes no damage

##### 4.1 Determine Critical Effect
If the Strike is a Critical Success, any Weapon Damage Die are treated as they rolled the maximum they could possibly Roll.

Additional Damage Dice such as Bleed Damage from [[Vital Strike]] are not treated as they rolled maximum.

### 5. Apply Damage to the defender
The total damage is then applied to the defender.

##### 5.1 Apply defender's Damage Reduction
Any Damage Reduction or Armor Value from the defender reduces the total damage value. [[Damage Types#Physical Damage | Physical Damage]] is always reduced first.

##### 5.2 Reduce defender's Hit Points
Reduce the defender's Hit Points by the remaining damage, if any. [[Temporary Hit Points]] are always reduced before Hit Points.

##### 5.3 Resolve Damage effects
Resolve all effects that would happen upon dealing damage. An effect that happens upon Dealing Damage is only resolved if the defender lost one or more Hit Points from the Attack.
