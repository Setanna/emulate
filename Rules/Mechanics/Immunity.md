# Immunity
Each Immunity corresponds to a specific Trait in Emulate, and may also include associated Conditions or Damage Types.

When you gain an Immunity, the following apply:

### Trait Protection
You cannot be affected by any source with that Trait.
Any ongoing effects with that Trait immediately end.

### Condition Protection
If the Immunity lists any Conditions:
 - You cannot gain those Conditions.
 - If you already have them, they are removed.

### Damage Type Protection
If the Immunity lists any Damage Types, you cannot take damage of those types.

### Immunities

##### Fear
- Frightened Condition

##### Fire
- [[Damage Types | Fire]] Damage.
- [[Burning]] Condition.
