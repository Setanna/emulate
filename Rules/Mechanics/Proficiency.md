# Proficiency
Proficiency determines what a character is good at and what a character is bad at. A character can be proficient in a varied amount of things:
 - Armors
 - Shields
 - Weapons
 - Skills
 - Languages

### Armor
There are 3 types of Armor of which you can be be proficient in: Light, Medium and Heavy.

<br>

While you are wearing Armor without the proficiency you incur a [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Proficiency | Proficiency]] Penalty to your Armor Value based on the Armor Type:
- Light Armor incurs a -1 [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Proficiency | Proficiency]] Penalty
- Medium Armor incurs a -2 [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Proficiency | Proficiency]] Penalty
- Heavy Armor incurs a -3 [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Proficiency | Proficiency]] Penalty

### Shields
There are 3 types of Shields of which you can be be proficient in: Light, Medium and Heavy.

<br>

> [!example|right] Example
> If you a wielding a [[Buckler]] while not having [[Light Shield Proficiency]]. You do not benefit from the [[Parrying]] Trait

While you are wielding a Shield without the proper proficiency you can not benefit from any of the following Traits the Shield Has:
- [[Parrying]]
- [[Bulwark]]
- [[Bastion]]
- [[Bash]]

### Weapons
There are 3 types of weapons; Simple, Advanced and Exotic

<br>

When making an attack Roll with a weapon of which the attacker is not proficient, they incurs a -2 [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Proficiency | Proficiency]] Penalty to Attack Rolls.

### Skills
All your skills you don't have [[Skill Proficiency]] in, incurs a -2 [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Proficiency | Proficiency]] Penalty. 

### Languages
Languages are build up of 3 proficiencies: Untrained, Basic and Fluent.

<br>

**Untrained:** Creatures untrained in a language can't understand, speak, write or read it.

<br>

**Basic:** Creatures with a basic understanding of a language, as such they can understand, speak, write or read most of it, some sayings, words and dialects might go over their head. Basic Speech tends to be a bit slower with several stops to think of the right words, as such Creatures using the language takes a -2 [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Proficiency | Proficiency]] Penalty to all Rolls and DCs for  [[Language]] Actions.

<br>

**Fluent:** Creatures fluent in a language, can speak it without thinking twice and can most common sayings and dialects.
