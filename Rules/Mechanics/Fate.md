# Fate
Fate favors the bold—Fate is a vital part of Emulate, empowering each Player Character to shape the flow of destiny itself.

<br>

When choosing to play Emulate, the Players and the Game Master must all agree on how big a role Fate will play in their game. Will their characters be nobodies just fighting for survival and the next bag of coin? Or will they be heroes whoose names will be spoken of for generations.

<br>

Below are several templates for how Fate works in the game and how it affect the Player Characters:

### Fateless
*No one is coming to save the Player Characters. Survive with wit, cunning or die trying*

<br>

Fate Dice and all content with the [[Fate]] Trait is removed from the game.

### Unfavored
*In a world of fated heroes and prophecies, the Player Characters are neither*

<br>

Player Characters start the game without any Fate Dice.

### Preordained
*Fate is real, but it belongs to greater powers; the world moves without their say*

<br>

Player Characters start the game with 3 Fate Dice. All content with the [[Fate]] Trait except for the [[Twist of Fate]] Action is removed from the game.

### Fated
*Fate gives the Player Characters a chance, but it is up to them to seize it*

<br>

Player Characters start the game with 3 Fate Dice. The Game Master can expend these Fate Dice for the Player Characters and use the [[Twist of Fate]] Action for them, even if they lack the Action.

<br>

### Destined
*Whether it is an untold prophecy or the favor of an amused god, the Player Characters always have things go their way*

<br>

Player Characters start the game with 6 Fate Dice and all gain the [[Twist of Fate]] Action.

## Fate Dice
Fate Dice are a pool of dice used to change the result of an Action. Each Player Character Regains one expended Fate Die at the end of a [[Resting | Long Rest]]. Fate Dice start at a d4, and can be increased and altered by Talents or Characteristics.

### Expending Fate Dice
Dependent on whether the world is Destined or Fated a Player or Game Master can expend Fate Dice using the [[Twist of Fate]] Action.
