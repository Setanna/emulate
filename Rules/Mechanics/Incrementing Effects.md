# Incrementing Effects
In some cases during the play of Emulate, a situation can occur of which a modifier or duration does not work in the current time frame. As such there is always the option of incrementing duration or interval based effects.

## Incremental Durations
When tracking a creature in [[Weather | Heavy Rain]], the trackers take a -8 [[Circumstance]] Penalty for each hour the creature's track have been in the rain. However if the trackers are only an half hour behind, they would suffer no penalties despite the environmental conditions. For such cases it is better to split up the -8 [[Circumstance]] Penalty per hour up to a -4 [[Circumstance]] Penalty each half hour, to better suit the  current time frame and situation.

## Incremental Modifiers
Some Modifiers are based on how far away a source is or different factors.  Such as when a creature takes a -4 [[Circumstance]] Penalty for each 10 meters in [[Weather | Dense Fog]], but the source is only 5 meters away. In such a situation the creature would suffer no penalties despite the environmental conditions. For such cases it is better to split up the -4 [[Circumstance]] Penalty per 10 meters to a -2 [[Circumstance]] Penalty per 5 meters..
