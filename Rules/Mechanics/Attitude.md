# Attitude
In the system of Emulate, there are many allies and foes. To determine how Player Characters interact with the world and how the world interact back, the Attitude System is there to quickly and simply help with relations and how helpful an ally might be, or how far a foe might go to end the Player character.

<br>

There are 7 Attitudes, that defines the range of hatred or love, Creatures can feel for each other.

| Attitude    | Charisma DC Modifier | Example                                                                                  |
| ----------- |:--------------------:| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Resentful   |          +4          | Mutually assured destruction. Willing to risk everything to ruin the Creature's life     |
| Hostile     |          +2          | Will chase away, jail or kill the creature. Will not risk their life if they can help it |
| Distant     |          +1          | Will not offer help and will end conversations quickly                                   |
| Indifferent |          0           | No real difference                                                                       |
| Kind        |          -1          | Might offer small amounts of help and long conversations                                 |
| Friendly    |          -2          | Might offer more help if it is not a direct threat on their life or livelihood           |
| Loyal       |          -4          | Will risk life and livelihood for the creature                                           | 

<br>

While an Attitude is a great guideline for how characters might act, they are not set in stone. A Paladin that would always risk his life to save others, might still risk his life to save Innocent Bystanders even if they are from the Enemy Nation and he is Distant against them.
