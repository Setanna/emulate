# Stat Based Variables
Many Talents and Spells have varied effects depending on your Stat. Whenever anything refers to your stat the minimum is always 1. Whenever an effect would be based on a multiple of your Stat, the minimum is also increased.

<br>

> ### Example
> A Kobold with -2 Constitution gains the a Fatigued Condition with a value of 3
> Fatigue states that a creature gains the Exhausted Condition if they ever exceed twice their Constitution.
> The Kobolds Constitution is negative, so the minimum is 1, but the effect refers to twice their Constitution, so the minimum is instead 2.
