# Allies and Enemies
In Emulate, Talents, Spells and the like, often refer to Allies or Enemies.

## Allies
Any Creature with an [[Attitude | Kind]] or better toward you is considered an Ally.

## Enemies
Any Creature with an [[Attitude | Distant]] or worse toward you is considered an Enemy.
