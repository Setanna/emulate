# Scatter
In Emulate, sometimes things can randomly go flying. The Scatter Mechanic allows the GM and Actions to determine where an item goes flying.

### Nearby
A Nearby Scatter is determined by 1d8 for direction and 1d6 for distance.

### Moderate
A Moderate Scatter is determined by 1d8 for direction and 2d6 for distance.

### Large
A Large Scatter is determined by 1d8 for direction and 4d6 for distance.
