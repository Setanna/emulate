# Multiplication
In Emulate many things can multiply your effect or similar.

# Division
In Emulate many things can reduce your effects or similar by dividing it. If you would ever divide a number, always round up.

> **Example**
> If you have an Armor Value of 4
> You would reduce all Physical Damage by your Armor Value
> And reduce all Elemental Damage by one third of your Armor Value
> This would mean you would reduce all Physical Damage by 4 and all Elemental Damage by 2
