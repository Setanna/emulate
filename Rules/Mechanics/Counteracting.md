# Counteracting
In the system of Emulate, several effects and conditions can come to clash. Counteracting is the rules for how to handle one effect overpowering the other.

## Counteracting Actions
When an Action is counteracted, skip the resolve step for that Action. *After Triggers still 
get triggered if their requirements are met.*

## Counteracting Conditions
Conditions have two different values, a static value or a die value.

### Counteracting Die Conditions
When counteracting Die Conditions, the steps of each Condition are subtracted from each other, and the greater of the Conditions remains.

<br>

> ##### Example
> 1. A Kobold with the [[Drenched | Drenched (d10)]] Condition gains the [[Burning | Burning (d6)]] Condition.
> 2. The new condition has a d6 Effect Die, reducing the Kobolds [[Drenched]] Condition by 2 steps.
> 3. The Kobolds new [[Drenched]] Condition now has a d6 Effect Die.

<br>

### Counteracting Static Conditions
Static Value Conditions are a lot simpler as they have numeric values. As such simply subtract the values from each other, and the greater of the Conditions remains.
