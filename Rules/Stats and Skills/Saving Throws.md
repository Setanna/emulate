# Saving Throws
A creature has 3 saving throws: Reflex, Fortitude and Will.

### Reflex
Reflex is a Dexterity based Roll used to avoid danger through ones reflexes.

### Fortitude
Fortitude is a Constitution based Roll used to resist diseases, mitigate damage, and endure poisons through sheer toughness and resilience.

### Will
Will is a Wisdom based Roll used to resist temptations, mental intrusions and maintain focus in the face of adversity. 

## Recovery Saving Throws
A Recovery Saving Throw is a Saving Throw you make after failing an initial Saving Throw against an effect, allowing you to Roll again at a later time to try to end that effect. When making a Recovery Saving Throw, you Roll once and compare the result to all relevant effects as defined in the parentheses of the Recovery Save.

A Recovery Saving Throw can never result in a worse degree of success than the original Saving Throw. For example, if the original Saving Throw was a Failure, the Recovery Saving Throw can never turn it into a Critical Failure.

### Single Recovery Save
A Single Recovery Save is used for effects that require you to Roll once at a specific interval, such as the daily Recovery Save for a disease. This is shown in the disease’s description as:
- At the end of each day, Roll a Recovery Save (Disease Name)

This means you Roll a single Recovery Saving Throw of whatever type that disease requires.

### Saving Throw Recovery Save
A Saving Throw Recovery Save is commonly used by talents or spells that allow a character to break free of certain effects. This is shown in the talent or spell as:
- Roll a Recovery Save (Will)

This means you Roll a single Will Saving Throw, applying the result to all effects that require a Will Save to end.

### Trait Recovery Save
A Trait Recovery Save works similarly to a Saving Throw Recovery Save but uses a Trait instead of a Saving Throw type. For example:
- Roll a Recovery Save (Fear)

This means you Roll a single Recovery Saving Throw against all effects with the Fear Trait. If this would require Saving Throws of different types (such as Reflex, Will, or Fortitude), then Roll one Recovery Saving Throw for each type required.

> ##### Example
> A Human is affected by two DC 14 Fear Spells that gives him frightened 1 and frightened 2.
> The Human casts [[Halo of Courage]] and makes a Recovery (Will) Saving Throw.
> The Human rolls a 15 (With the bonuses from [[Halo of Courage]]).
> The Human compares the new Roll against each of the Spells and treats the spell as if they had originally rolled the new result.
> The spells state that on a Success nothing happens.
> The Human removes any Frightened Condition from the spell as he Succeeded the Saving Throw
