# Skills
Each creature has a set of skills based on the 6 [[Stats]].

## Take 10 and 20
When making a Skill Roll outside of an [[Encounters | Encounter]], one can elect to forego the Roll and simply treat the Skill Roll as if the die had rolled 10.

<br>

Alternatively the roll can be treated as if the die had rolled 20, but the task would time 20 times as long. If the task takes 3 actions, taking 20 would make the action take 60 actions or 12 rounds. 

<br>

Whenever you take 10 or 20, the action used gains the [[Focus]] Trait.

## Skill Examples

| ----- | ------------------------------------------------ |

### Constitution
Constitution lacks any skills as resisting diseases or alcohol is considered a Fortitude Saving Throw.
