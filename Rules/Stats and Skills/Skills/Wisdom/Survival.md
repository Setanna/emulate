# Survival (Wis)
Survival is used to handle yourself in the wilderness, traversing the wilds, seeking shelter and food.

### Survival Modifiers
| Survival Modifiers                                |   DC Modifier    |
| ------------------------------------------------- |:----------------:|
| For each three creatures in a group being tracked |        -1        |
| Creature's Size Bonus to Stealth                  |      Varies      |
| Every 24 hours since the trail was made           |        +1        |
| Every hour of rain                                |        +1        |
| Fresh Snow                                        |        +5        |
