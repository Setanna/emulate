# Medicine (Wis)
Medicine is used to tend to the ailments or others and treat wounds.

### Medicine Proficiency
You gain the following benefits when having a Proficiency Bonus equal to or higher than the given value.

#### +1 Proficiency Bonus
When you use the [[Treat Wounds]] Action, you can suffer a -4 [[Circumstance]] Penalty to remove the [[Rules/Traits/Item| Item (Medicine Kit)]] Trait from the Action.
