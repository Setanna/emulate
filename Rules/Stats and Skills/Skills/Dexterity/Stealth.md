# Stealth (Dex)
Stealth is used to hide from other creatures, typically done against the creature's Perception DC or sometimes a Perception Roll.

### Stealth Modifier

| Stealth Modifier                                                         | Modifier | Sense   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |:--------:| ------- |
| Standing out in the open in [[Lighting \| Normal Lighting ]] or brighter |   -30    | Sight   |
| [[Benefiting from Cover]]                                                |   +1-4   | Sight   |
| [[Invisible]]                                                            |   +10    | Sight   |
| Not moving while [[Invisible]]                                           |   +20    | Sight   |
| A noisy environment such as in battle                                    |    +5    | Hearing |
| Walking over a noisy surface (like gravel or glass shards)               |  +2-10   | Hearing |
