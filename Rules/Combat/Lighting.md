# Lighting
Lighting can effect a creatures [[Perception | Perception (Sight)]].


| Lighting          | Modifier    | 
| ----------------- | ----------- |
| Complete Darkness | [[Blinded]] |
| Dim Light         | -2          |
| Normal Light      | 0           |
| Bright Light      | +2          |
| Blinding Light    | [[Blinded]] |

### Complete Darkness
Complete Darkness concurs the [[Blinded]] condition.

### Dim Light
Dim Light concurs a -2 Circumstance Penalty on [[Perception | Perception (Sight)]] and any [[Skills#Strength | Strength ]] or [[Skills#Dexterity | Dexterity ]] based rolls.

### Bright Light
Bright Light concurs a bonus on [[Perception | Perception (Sight)]].

### Blinding Light
Blinding Light concurs the [[Blinded]] condition.
