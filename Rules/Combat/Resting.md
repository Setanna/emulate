# Resting
Resting defines the downtime of regaining ones strength, this includes Short Rests, Long Rests and the preparation following rests.

## Short Rest
A Short Rest is defined as 1 hour of uninterrupted light activity, such as reading, talking, eating, or standing watch.

### Finishing a Short Rest
After finishing a Short Rest you regain a single Hit Point.

### Interrupting a Short Rest
A Long Rest can be interrupted by the following:
 - Rolling [[Initiative]]
 - Casting a spell
 - Taking damage
 - 10 minutes of walking or other physical activity


## Long Rest
A Long Rest is defined as 8 hours of uninterrupted downtime, of which 6 must be spent sleeping, the remaining 2 can be spent on light activity, such as reading, talking, eating, or standing watch.

<br>

### Finishing a Long Rest
After finishing rest you roll your Racial Hit Die and regain a number of hit points equal to the result, to a minimum of your Constitution.

### Interrupting a Long Rest
A Long Rest can be interrupted by the following:
 - Rolling [[Initiative]]
 - Casting a spell
 - Taking damage
 - half an hour of walking or other physical activity

If you rested at least 1 hour before the interruption you gain the benefits of a Short Rest.

<br>

You can resume an interrupted Long Rest after an interruption, doing so increases the time needed to gain the benefits by 1 Hour.

## Preparation 
After a Rest there is a Preparation phase which lasts 1 hour. During the Preparation phase the following is assumed to be taken care of:
 - Clear Camp
 - Gain Spell Points
 - Gear up
