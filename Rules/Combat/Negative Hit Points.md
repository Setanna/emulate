# Negative Hit Points
When a creature reaches Negative Hit Points they fall [[Unconscious]] and at the start of their turn must Roll a [[Saving Throws  | Fortitude]] Save or Take 1 Damage that can't be reduced. The DC for the Saving Throw is 10 + The Negative Hit Points.

<br>

When a creature's Negative Hit Points exceeds their Racial Hit Die, the creature dies.
