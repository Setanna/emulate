# Line of Sight

Many Actions or Spells require line of sight to their target. This requires the one using the Action or Spell has the ability to perceive their surroundings in one way or another. Obstructions in Line of Sight can be determined in the [[Benefitting from Cover]] section.

<br>

To target a creature with a spell or attack, you must know which square the creature resides in. If a creature doesn't benefit from [[Benefitting from Cover#Full Cover| Full Cover]] or [[Invisibility]] you know which square it is in using sight. If you're [[Blinded]] you must use hearing to determine their square as described in [[Perception]].
