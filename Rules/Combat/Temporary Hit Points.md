# Temporary Hit Points
Temporary Hit Points work similarly to Hit Points, but are used before any damage is taken to Hit Points. You can not gain several instances of Temporary Hit Points, the larger of them are applied.

> ### Example
> 1. A Kobold with 3 Temporary Hit Points and 5 Hit Points takes 7 Damage from a Human
> 2. The kobold's Temporary Hit Points are applied first, taking 3 of the 7 Damage.
> 3. The Kobold's Hit Points then take the rest of the 4 damage leaving the Kobold at 1 Hit Point.
