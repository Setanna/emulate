# Rolls
A Roll is defined as rolling a d20 to determine whether or not something succeeds on a Critical Fail; Fail; Success or Critical Success basis. While Damage Rolls have Roll in the name they do not count as Rolls.

### Ability Score Based Rolls
Ability Score Based Rolls are defined as regular Roll but where a certain Ability Score is added such as Stealth using Dexterity.

### Rolling against a DC
A DC is a Difficulty Check, the Roll needs to be equal or higher than the Roll to succeed. Most tasks have a Static DC determined by the Game Master. There are scenarios where a Roll is made against another Creatures DC such as rolling Intimidation against Will DC.
