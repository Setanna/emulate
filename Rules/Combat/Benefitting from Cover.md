# Cover
There are 4 types of cover; Quarter Cover, Half Cover, Three-Quarter Cover and Total Cover.

### Quarter Cover
![[quarter_cover.png]]{width=100%}

Quarter Cover grants you a +1 [[Cover]] Bonus.

<br>

**Examples**
- Knee down cover.
- Standing behind a knee high wall.
- Standing next to a corner.

### Half Cover
![[half_cover.png]]{width=100%}

Half Cover grants you a +2 [[Cover]] Bonus.

<br>

**Examples**
- Waist down cover.
- Standing behind a flipped over table.
- Attacking from behind a corner.

### Three-Quarters Cover
![[three_quarter_cover.png]]{width=100%}

Three-Quarter Cover grants you a +4 [[Cover]] Bonus.

**Examples**
- Shoulder down cover.
- Crouched behind a flipped over table.
- Peeking out from a Corner

### Full Cover
![[full_cover.png]]{width=100%}

Full Cover makes it so you can't be targeted by attacks, take no effects from sources with the [[Blast]] trait and can't be perceived by sight.

**Examples**
- Entire body cover.
- Hiding behind a flipped over table.
- Up against the wall of a corner.

## Determining Cover
Cover is determined when a line can't be drawn directly from the source to the target. Depending on the size of the object or creature standing between the source and the target a type of cover is granted.

<br>

A creature standing behind another creature of same size or larger gains a Half Cover Bonus. Standing behind a creature of a size smaller only grants a Quarter Cover Bonus, while standing behind a creature of two sizes smaller or less grants no cover bonus.
