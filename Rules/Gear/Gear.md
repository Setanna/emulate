# Gear
Emulate is filled with a wide variety of gear (Not yet true). 
The following is the rules for the value of items, acqusition of items and more (Maybe).

### Value
The value of an Item is determined in the top right corner next to it's name.
The value is always by coins:
- GC: [[Gold Coin | Gold Coins]]
- SC: [[Silver Coin | Silver Coins]]
- CC: [[Copper Coin | Copper Coins]]

### Variation
Some gear has Variations and as such are split up, each Variation with it's own Value and system rules.
An example is the [[Tattoos of Triumph]].

### Weight
Weight determines how much a piece of Gear weighs when carried by a Creature. 

### Acquisition
There are many was to Acquire Gear:
- Purchasing Gear using Coins
- Trading Gear for other Gear
- Rewards for completing quests
- Theft
- Graverobbing
