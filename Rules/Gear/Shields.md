# Shields
Shields grant a **SV (Shield Value)**, that function similarly to armor if you use the shield passively or as it's own reaction if you use the shield reactively. At the start of your turn, you decide if you want to use your shield reactively or passively. 

#### Passively
When using a shield passively, add the **Shield Value** to your **Armor Value**.

#### Reactively
When using a shield reactively, you gain no bonus against attacks, but can use your reaction to increase your **[[Making an Attack#Rolling to Hit| HC]]** by an amount equal to your **SV** against any attack. 

<br>

Additionally if the attack still hits, you get to reduce any **[[Damage Types#Physical Damage | physical damage]]** by an amount equal to your **SV**.

<br>

Light shields can be used to parry.
