# Negative Hit Points
When a creature reaches 0 Hit Points or less they fall [[Unconscious]] and at the start of their turn must Roll a [[Saving Throws  | Fortitude]] Save or Take 1 Damage that can't be reduced. The DC for the Saving Throw is 10 + the Creature's current Negative Hit Points.

<br>

When a creature's Negative Hit Points exceeds their Maximum Negative Hit Points, the creature dies. A creature's Maximum Negative Hit Points is half their Racial Hit Die.

>[!info|right] Example
>A Human with a d8 Hit Die would have a Maximum Negative Hit Points value of 4
