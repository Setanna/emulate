# Combat
In Emulate, Combat is handled in a turn based structure as shown below:

### Rolling Initiative
Each Creature in combat rolls a [[Perception]] Skill Check. Each creature is then ordered from highest Roll to lowest. In cases of a tie the creature with the highest Wisdom goes first, if the Wisdom is tied, Roll a die to determine who goes first.

<br>

The list of creature and their Initiative is called the Turn Order and decides what creature goes first, second and so on.

### Round
A round is made up of all the creatures in the Turn Order, when each creature has had their turn a round has concluded, in which a new round will start from the top of the Turn Order. 

### Turn
A Creature's turn is structured as shown below:

##### Start of Turn
At the start of its turn, a creature resets its time, gaining 6 Seconds and 1 Split Second. These Seconds can be spent on various activities, such as attacking, casting spells, or other actions available to the creature. Likewise, the Split Second can be used in response to specific triggers as defined by reactions, actions and talents.

<br>

Additionally, all Start of Turn effects trigger. The Creature whose turn it is decides the order if it is needed.

##### Actions
The Creature decides what actions it uses. Resolve each action as shown in [[Taking an Action]].

##### End of Turn
The Creature's Turn ends. Any unspent Seconds are lost at the end of the creature's turn, and any unused Split Seconds from the previous round are also lost at the start of the creature's turn.

<br>

Additionally, all End of Turn effects trigger. The Creature whose turn it is decides the order if it is needed.
