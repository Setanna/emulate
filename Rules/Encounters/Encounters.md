# Encounters
An encounter is a structured amount of time where the Player Character's are considered to be engaged in some form of activity. Encounters come in 3 types:

 - Obstacle Encounters
 - Combat Encounters
 - Social Encounters

##### Obstacle Encounters
Obstacle Encounters are Encounters where the Player Characters have to use their abilities to bypass an obstacle, whether a ruined bridge or a large citadel wall.

##### Combat Encounters
Combat Encounters are Encounters are Encounters where the Player Character fight enemies in a structured turn based order.

##### Social Encounters
Social Encounters are Encounters are Encounters where the Player Character's have to gain information, favours or allies using bargaining, intimidation or deception.

<br>

Some Encounters can be made up of several smaller Encounters of different types. Such as a Party of Adventurers trying to get into a blocked off mine shaft only to find Kobolds hiding ready to attack.

Some Encounters can even be incredibly long, such as the party traversing through an ancient tomb, where they must be on their guard at all times.
