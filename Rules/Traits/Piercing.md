# Piercing
Attacks made using Weapons with the Piercing Trait reduces the Defender's Combined Armor and Shield Value for that Attack. This reduction is only applied to the amount of Damage that the Armor and Shield would reduce. The amount of reduction is equal to the value of this trait, as shown in it's parenthesis. 

<br>

When a Weapon with the Piercing Trait Hits with a Critical Success double its Piercing Value.
