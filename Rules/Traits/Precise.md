# Precise
A Weapon or Spell with the Precise Trait can not gain the [[Reckless]] Trait. When you make an [[Making an Attack | Attack]] the Defender's Armor and Shield Value is applied to their Hit Class instead of reducing any Damage Taken from any of the [[Making an Attack | Attack]].

<br>

If you take an Action with the Precise Trait and the Action makes you take an [[Making an Attack | Attack]], the Weapon or Spell gains the Precise Trait for the duration of that [[Making an Attack | Attack]].
