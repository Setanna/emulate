# Imprinted
A creature affected by an effect with the Imprinted Trait only resolves the initial Saving Throw once per [[Resting#Preparation | Preparation]]. The result of that Saving Throw is recorded for that creature and reused for all subsequent interactions with that effect during the same [[Resting#Preparation | Preparation]].

<br>

While the creature remains subject to the effect, it does not attempt additional Saving Throws against that same instance of the effect. If the creature leaves and later re-enters the area during the same Preparation, it does not reroll; instead, it reuses its stored result.

<br>

The stored result is cleared at the start of a new [[Resting#Preparation | Preparation]].
