# Propulsive
A Weapon with the Propulsive Trait adds Strength to Damage Rolls instead of Dexterity.

<br>

Some Weapons with the Propulsive Trait also have a numeric value, if the Propulsive Trait has such a value, the given value is the Strength Required to use the weapon. If a creature has less Strength than the given value, it gains a [[Circumstance]] Penalty on all Attack Rolls with the Weapon. The [[Circumstance]] Penalty is equal to the difference of their Strength Stat and Propulsive Trait.

<br>

> ### Example
> A Kobold with -2 Strength tries to Attack with a Shortbow.
> The Shortbow has a Propulsive 1 Trait.
> The Difference between the Kobold's Strength and Propulsive Trait is 3.
> The Kobold takes a -3 [[Circumstance]] Penalty on all Attack Rolls with the Shortbow.
