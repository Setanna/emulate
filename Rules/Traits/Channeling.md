# Channeling
Actions or Talents with the Channeling Trait automatically gains the [[Focus]] Trait. These Actions and Talents also require focus and concentration, but not just for the Action on it, for the entire duration. If for any reason the creature can not meet the Requirements of using a [[Focus]] Action or Talent, the creature loses all active Channeling Actions or Talents.
