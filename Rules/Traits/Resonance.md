# Resonance
A Talent with the Resonance Trait, has increased benefits dependent on a Requirement met in the Resonance Section of the talent. Most commonly the Talent has increased benefits for each Talent you have that has the Resonant Trait as a Requirement.
*This includes all Requirements from [[Requirements#Cascading Requirements | Cascading Requirements]].*
