# Bastion
Shields with the Bastion Trait are large enough for others to gain cover behind. When you provide cover for others, instead of [[Benefiting from Cover#Half Cover| Half Cover]] they would instead gain [[Benefiting from Cover#Three-Quarter Cover| Three-Quarters Cover]].

<br>

Additionally Shields with the Bastion Trait don't grant a reaction and can only be used to [[Take Cover]] or [[Block]] Reaction.
