# Bastion
Armor or Shields with the Bastion Trait are large enough for others to gain cover behind. When you provide cover for others, instead of [[Benefitting from Cover#Half Cover | Half Cover]] they would instead gain [[Benefitting from Cover#Three-Quarter Cover| Three-Quarters Cover]].

<br>

If you would benefit from two or more sources with the [[Bastion]] Trait, increase the cover gained by 1 in addition to [[Benefitting from Cover#Three-Quarter Cover| Three-Quarters Cover]].
