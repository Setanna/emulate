# Heavy
Weapons with the Heavy Trait are unwieldy and increase the [[Circumstance]] Penalty from [[Multiattack]] by 2 for Attack Rolls made with this Weapon.

<br>

Additionally when using Heavy Weapons for the [[Rules/Traits/Attack | Attack]] Action, the Action Cost is increased by one; and the Attacker can give the [[Rules/Actions/Base Actions/Attacks/Attack | Attack]] Action the [[Reckless]] Trait without increasing the Action Cost.
