# Heavy
Weapons with the Heavy Trait are unwieldy and powerful. When making an [[Making an Attack | Attack]] with a Heavy Weapon you gain the following bonuses and penalties:
 - You suffer a -2 [[Circumstance]] Penalty on Attack Rolls unless wielded in two hands.
 - You add one and a half times your Strength to the Damage Roll instead of just your Strength.

Additionally all Heavy Weapons also increase the Second Cost of all [[Attack]] Actions made with Heavy Weapons by one Second. When making several attacks with different Heavy Weapons, such as with [[Multiattack]], increase the Second Cost of the Action for each Weapon to a maximum of 2 additional Seconds.

<br>

[[Making an Attack | Attacks]] made with Heavy Weapons can not gain the [[Accurate]] Trait.
