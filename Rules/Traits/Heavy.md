# Heavy
Weapons with the Heavy Trait are unwieldy and powerful. When wielding a Heavy Weapon, you take a -2 [[Circumstance]] Penalty on all Attack Rolls made with the weapon, unless it is wielded in two hands. 
You add twice your Strength to the Damage Rolls of Heavy Weapons instead of just your Strength.

<br>

Additionally when using Heavy Weapons for any Action with the [[Attack]] Trait, the cost is increased by one Second and the Action gains the [[Reckless]] Trait.
