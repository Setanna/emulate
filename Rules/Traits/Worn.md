# Worn
Gear with the Worn Trait are worn by creatures. The Value of the Trait determines how it is worn as well as how many of them can we worn as shown below:

### Head
Gear with the Worn (Head) Trait are items such as helmets, masks and hats. 

<br>

You can only wear one item with the Worn (Head) Trait.

### Eyes
Gear with the Worn (Eyes) Trait are items such as glasses or lenses.

<br>

You can only wear one item with the Worn (Eyes) Trait.

### Shoulders
Gear with the Worn (Shoulders) Trait are items such as robes, cloaks and capes.

<br>

You can only wear one item with the Worn (Shoulders) Trait.

### Neck
Gear with the Worn (Neck) Trait are items such as amulets and necklaces.

<br>

You can only wear two items with the Worn (Neck) Trait.

### Chest
Gear with the Worn (Chest) Trait are items such as blouses, vests and shirts.

<br>

You can only wear two items with the Worn (Chest) Trait.

### Legs
Gear with the Worn (Legs) Trait are items such as pants and trousers.

<br>

You can only wear two items with the Worn (Legs) Trait.

### Armor
Gear with the Worn (Armor) Trait are items such as scale mail and full plates.

<br>

You can only wear one item with the Worn (Armor) Trait.

### Shields
Gear with the Worn (Shields) Trait are items such as bucklers and heater shields.

<br>

You can only wear two items with the Worn (Shields) Trait.

### Belt
Gear with the Worn (Belt) Trait are items such as belts and sashes.

<br>

You can only wear two items with the Worn (Belt) Trait.

### Wrist
Gear with the Worn (Wrist) Trait are items such as bracers and bracelets.

<br>

You can only wear two items with the Worn (Wrist) Trait.

### Hands
Gear with the Worn (Hand) Trait are items such as gloves and gauntlets.

<br>

You can only wear two items with the Worn (Hands) Trait.

### Rings
Gear with the Worn (Rings) Trait are items such as rings and bands.

<br>

You can only wear four items with the Worn (Rings) Trait.

### Feet
Gear with the Worn (Feet) Trait are items such as boots and greaves.

<br>

You can only wear one item with the Worn (Feet) Trait.
