# Reckless
An [[Rules/Traits/Attack | Attack]] Action with the Reckless Trait can not benefit from the [[Precise]] Trait, and instead any Defender takes a [[Circumstance]] Penalty to their Defensive Reaction Rolls against the [[Rules/Traits/Attack | Attack]] Action. The Penalty is equal to your Strength.
