# Bonded
Social Bonds with the Bonded Trait require you to choose a number of creatures to gain any benefit from the Social Bond, and the chosen creatures must also have the Social Bond and choose you as their chosen creature.

<br>

In some cases instead of creatures, an establishment or area can be chosen instead. In such cases the chosen option does not need to have the Social Bond for you to gain any benfit.
