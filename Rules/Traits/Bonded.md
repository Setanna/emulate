# Bonded
A Social Bond with the Bonded Trait requires you to choose one or more eligible targets. These are usually creatures, though some bonds may allow an establishment, location, or similar entity instead.

Each Social Bond keeps its own list of Bonded targets.  Only the targets chosen for that specific Social Bond provide its benefits.

For creature targets, the bond must be mutual:
- You must choose the creature, and
- The creature must have the same Social Bond and choose you.

After a [[Resting | Long Rest]] with a creature, you may add them as a Bonded target for any Social Bond you possess, as long as all requirements for that bond are met.

Non-creature targets do not need to reciprocate the bond.
