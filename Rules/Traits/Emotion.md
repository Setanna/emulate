# Emotion
A source with the Emotion Trait works of ones Emotion, pushing soldiers to the edge or angering the enemy into a trap.

<br>

There are the following Subcategories of Emotions:

### Anger
Unbridled and uncontained spark that can turn into a roaring flame

### Fear
There is always so much to fear

### Joy
Pure and untainted, joy is the emotion most live for.

### Pain
With physical pain, emotional stress can quickly follow.
