# Emotion
A source with the Emotion Trait works of ones Emotion, pushing soldiers to the edge or angering the enemy into a trap.

<br>

There are the following Subcategories of Emotions:

### Anger
Unbridled and uncontained spark that can turn into a roaring flame

### Awe
The hush before thunder, the breath caught in wonder—when faced with the grand, the mind kneels

### Disgust
A gut-deep revulsion, the instinct to recoil from what should not be

### Fear
It waits behind every corner, whispering caution or cowardice

### Hope
Hope does not promise ease, only direction

### Joy
Pure and untainted, joy is the emotion most live for. It uplifts, ignites, and connects

### Pain
Pain humbles the proud and forges the rest

### Sorrow
Some mornings, the sun rises—but doesn’t feel warm
