# Electricity
Sources with the Electricity Trait deal Electricity damage. When rolling for Attacks with the [[Electricity]] Trait, the Attack Roll gains a Circumstance Bonus against Targets wearing Armor with the [[Material | Material (Steel)]] trait. When rolling for Reflex Saving Throws against sources with the [[Electricity]] .The Circumstance Bonus is equal to their Armor Value.
