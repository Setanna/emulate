# Thrown

> [!Example|right] Range Example
> If the Trait's value is 5 Meters and your Strength Score is 3, the Weapon's Range is 8 Meters.
> <br>
> If the Trait's value is 5 Meters and your Strength Score is -5, the Weapon's Range is 0 Meters.

Weapons with the Thrown Trait can be used as Ranged Weapons with the [[Propulsive]] Trait. The range of the weapon is defined in this Trait's value and your strength (minimum 0). When making a [[Making a Strike | Strike]] Attack with Thrown Weapons, the Attack Roll can use Strength or Dexterity.

<br>

Additionally Weapons with the Thrown and [[Finesse]] Trait can still benefit from the [[Finesse]] Trait.
