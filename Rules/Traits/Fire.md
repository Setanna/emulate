# Fire
Sources with the Fire Trait deal fire damage and can ignite surroundings or even creatures. A creature that fails a Dexterity Saving Throw against a source with the Fire Trait; or takes damage from a Critical Attack with the Fire Trait, gains the [[Burning]] Condition with a value equal to the Fire Damage Die.

### Bright
Additionally all sources with the Fire Trait sheds [[Lighting | Normal Lighting]] for 5 meters and [[Lighting | Dim Lighting]] for another 5 meters.
