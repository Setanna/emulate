# Fire
Sources with the Fire Trait deal fire damage and can ignite surroundings or even creatures. A creature that fails a Dexterity Saving Throw against a source with the Fire Trait; or gets critically hit by an attack with the Fire Trait, catches fire as described below.

### Catching Fire
A creature or object that catches fire takes fire damage equal to the die of the same size as the source that caught them on fire or as described in the Fire Trait, at the start of their turn. Each turn after the creature or object has taken the Fire Damage, the Fire Damage Die increases by one step.

<br>

This damage can be prevented by using the [[Douse Flames]] action.

### Bright
Additionally all sources with the Fire Trait sheds [[Lighting | Normal Lighting]] for 5 meters and [[Lighting | Dim Lighting]] for another 5 meters.
