# Maneuver
Actions with the Maneuver Trait, aim to disorient, displace or disarm their opponents in one way or another. 

<br>

 If you take an action withe the Maneuver Trait and only use one hand you take a -4 [[Circumstance]] Penalty to any Roll of the Action. Additionally Actions with the Maneuver and [[Movement]] Trait do not provoke [[Attack of Opportunity]].
