# Aura
When a Creature enters the range of an Aura they gain the effects of said Aura for as long as they are in the Aura. 

All Effects with the Aura Trait also gain the [[Imprinted]] Trait. Additionally any Condition gained from an Aura gains the [[Persistent]] Trait as long as the effected creature is within the range of the Aura. The [[Persistent]] Trait's Value is equal to the Condition's Value.

The range of an Aura is defined in the parenthesis of this Trait. It is usually defined in meters, but can also be defined as a sense such as Sight or Smell.

If the Aura has no defined range, it instead has a range of 10 meters.
