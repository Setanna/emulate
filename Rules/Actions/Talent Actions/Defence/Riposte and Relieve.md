<h1> Riposte and Relieve <span style="margin-left: auto;"> Reaction </span> </h1>

[[Defence]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" />

___

**System:**
Whenever you are the target of a Melee Weapon Attack, you can parry the weapon by rolling a Strength Check against the Attack Roll.

<br>

**Critical Success:** You take no damage from the attack, and [[disarms]] the weapon.
**Success:** You take damage equal to the Attackers Strength which can be reduced by your Armor Value, and you [[disarm]] the weapon.
**Fail:** You take full damage from the attack.
