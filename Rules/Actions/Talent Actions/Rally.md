<h1> Rally <span style="margin-left: auto;"> Any Number of Actions</span> </h1>

[[Language]]{.trait}

[[Morale]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" />

___
*Your words rally the fallen to their feet and lets them face death with a sword in their hand.*

**System:**
You chose any number of allies that can hear you, up to an amount equal to your Charisma per Action used on this action. You choose one benefit to grant to all the targets until the end of the encounter:

<br>

##### Last Stand
Your allies gain the [[Indomitable]] Talent. Allies who already have the talent instead add your Charisma to their Constitution for determining when they die from Negative Hit Points.

##### Valiant Charge
Whenever your allies use the [[Charge]], they can charge an additional number of metres equal to your Charisma.

##### Shield Wall
Your allies wielding a Shield grant a Passive Shield Value of 1 to all adjacent allies. If the ally already has a Passive Shield Value, increase it by 1 to a maximum of your Charisma.
