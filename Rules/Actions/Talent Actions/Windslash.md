<h1> Windslash <span style="margin-left: auto;">1 Action</span> </h1>

[[Offence]]{.trait}

[[Flexible]]{.trait}

[[Stance]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** You haven't taken an Action with the [[Movement]] Trait.

___
*With a slash, the wind becomes your weapon, tearing through anything in its path.*

<br>

**System:**
All your Melee Weapons that deal Slashing Damage gain the [[Reach]] Trait with a value equal to your Strength.
