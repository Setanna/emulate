<h1> Wind Stance <span style="margin-left: auto;">1 Action</span> </h1>

[[Offence]]{.trait}

[[Flexible]]{.trait}

[[Stance]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Requirements:** You haven't taken an Action with the [[Movement]] Trait.

___
*In stillness, the wind amplifies your reach, striking where your weapons cannot.*

<br>

**System:**
All your Melee Weapons that deal Slashing Damage gain the [[Rules/Traits/Reach|Reach]] Trait with a value equal to your Strength.
