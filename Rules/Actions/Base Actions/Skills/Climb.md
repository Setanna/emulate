<h1> Climb <span style="margin-left: auto;"> 3 Actions </span> </h1>

[[Skill | Skill (Climb)]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" />

**Requirements:** Both hands free.
**Trigger:** You are [[Falling]].
___

**System:**
You roll a Climb Roll against the DC of the surface you are climbing. While climbing you are [[Flat-Footed]].

<br>

Alternatively you can use only one hand to climb, leaving the other hand free for a torch or weapon, doing so incurs a -4 [[Circumstance]] Penalty to the Climb Roll.

<br>

**Critical Success:** You move up to your speed.
**Success:** You move 1 meter.
**Fail:** You don't move.
**Critical Fail:** You [[Falling | Fall]].
