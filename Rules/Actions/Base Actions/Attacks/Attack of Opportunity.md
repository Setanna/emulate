<h1> Attack of Opportunity <span style="margin-left: auto;">Reaction</span> </h1>

[[Attack]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

**Trigger:** A creature within your Reach does any of the following:
- Takes an Action with the [[Movement]] Trait
- Takes the [[Interact]] Action.
- Makes a Ranged Weapon Attack
- Casts a Spell

___

<br>

**System:**
You make a [[Swing for the Fences]] Attack against the creature that triggered the Attack of Opportunity.
