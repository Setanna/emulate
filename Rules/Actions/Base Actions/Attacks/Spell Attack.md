<h1> Spell Attack <span style="margin-left: auto;">2 or 3 Actions</span> </h1>

[[Attack]]{.trait}

[[Arcane]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

___

**System:**
Spell Attacks are attacks granted by spells. The damage, range (melee or ranged) and the type of attack ([[Swing for the Fences]] or [[Target the Gaps]]) are all defined in the spell that granted the Spell Attack. If a spell doesn't define whether or not an attack can be used as [[Swing for the Fences]] or [[Target the Gaps]] Attack Action, it is assumed it can do either.
