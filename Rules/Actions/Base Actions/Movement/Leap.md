<h1> Leap <span style="margin-left: auto;">1-3 Actions</span> </h1>

[[Movement]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" /> 

___

**System:**
When leaping you can choose to do a Standing Leap or Running Leap. The height of your leap is 100 cm plus 10 times your Strength.

<br>

**Standing Leap (1 Action):**
With a Standing Leap you can move a number of metres equal to your Strength to a minimum of 1 meter.

<br>

**Running Leap (3 Actions):**
You move up to your speed in a straight line and at the end of the movement you jump a number of meters equal to half the number of meters moved.

<br>

> **Size**
> For each size above medium double the length and height of your jump.
> For each size below medium half the length and height of your jump.
