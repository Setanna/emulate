<h1> Raise Shield <span style="margin-left: auto;"> Reaction </span> </h1>

[[Defence]]{.trait}

[[Rules/Traits/Item| Item (Shield)]]{.trait}
 
<div style="clear:both" />

**Trigger:** You or an ally within reach is the target of a Weapon Attack and you're using your shield reactively.
___

**System:**
You lift your shield to block the attack. Until the start of your next turn you add your **Shield Value** to your **Armor Value**.

<br>

When Raising a Shield for your ally, the effect lats until the start of their next turn, and the **Shield Value** is added to their **Armor Value**.
