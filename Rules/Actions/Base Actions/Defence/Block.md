<h1> Block <span style="margin-left: auto;"> Reaction </span> </h1>

[[Defence]]{.trait}

[[Rules/Traits/Item| Item (Shield)]]{.trait}
 
<div style="clear:both" />

**Trigger:** You or an ally within reach is the target of a Weapon Attack and you're using your shield reactively.
___

**System:**
You lift your shield to block the attack. When blocking an attack you gain the following benefits against said attack:
- You add your **Shield Value** to your or your ally's **Hit Class**.
- You reduce any [[Damage Types#Physical Damage | Physical Damage]] to you or you ally, by your **Shield Value**.
- You reduce any [[Damage Types | Elemental Damage]] to you or you ally, by one third of your **Shield Value**, rounded up.
