<h1> Take Cover <span style="margin-left: auto;"> Reaction </span> </h1>

[[Defence]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" />

**Trigger:**  You are the target of a attack; or a source with the [[Blast]] trait
___

**System:**
While benefiting from cover you can crouch or move in a way to place you better behind cover; increase your cover by one step until you take your next action.

<br>

Alternatively this can be used as a single action without a trigger.
