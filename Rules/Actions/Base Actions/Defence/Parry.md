<h1> Parry <span style="margin-left: auto;"> Reaction </span> </h1>

[[Defence]]{.trait}

[[Rules/Traits/Item| Item (Weapon or Light; Medium Shield)]]{.trait}

[[Flexible]]{.trait}

<div style="clear:both" />

**Trigger:**  You or your ally is the target of a Melee Weapon Attack.
___

**System:**
You parry the weapon by rolling an Attack Roll against the attacker's Attack Roll.

<br>

**Critical Success:** The attack counts as a Miss and you can make an [[Attack of Opportunity]] against the Attacker as a Free Action.
**Success:** The attack counts as a Miss.
**Fail:** You reduce the damage taken by your Strength.
**Critical Fail:** You take full damage from the attack.
