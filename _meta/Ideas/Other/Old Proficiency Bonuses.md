### Climb Proficiency
You gain the following benefits when having a Proficiency Bonus equal to or higher than the given value.

#### +1 Proficiency Bonus
You can [[Skills#Take 10 and 20 | Take 10]] on Climb Rolls during an Encounter.

#### +2 Proficiency Bonus
When using [[Content/Actions/Base Actions/Skills/Climb|Climb]] and [[Swift Grip]] Actions you are not [[Flat-Footed]].

#### +3 Proficiency Bonus
You can use the [[Content/Actions/Base Actions/Skills/Climb|Climb]] and [[Swift Grip]] Actions  with one hand free without any penalties.

#### +4 Proficiency Bonus
You have an iron grip, you ignore penalties to Climb Roll from environmental, such as slippery surfaces, strong winds or icy surfaces.

#### +5 Proficiency Bonus
You gain a Climb Speed equal to half your speed rounded up. If you already have a Climb Speed increase it by 10.


### Stealth Proficiency
You gain the following benefits when having a Proficiency Bonus equal to or higher than the given value.

#### +1 Proficiency Bonus
Before taking the [[Hide]] Action as a Reaction you can take the [[Step]] Action to move into [[Benefiting from Cover | Cover]] as a Free Action.

#### +2 Proficiency Bonus
While using the [[From Cover to Cover]] Action, your Stealth Roll benefits from the Cover you left.

#### +3 Proficiency Bonus
You know exactly how to take advantage of a surprise. When taking the [[Sneak Attack]] Action you gain a [[Circumstance]] Bonus to the damage equal to your [[Rules/Bonuses and Penalties/Proficiency|Proficiency]] Bonus on Stealth.

#### +4 Proficiency Bonus
Your step is light as a feather. You gain no penalty to Stealth Rolls from walking over noisy surfaces.

#### +5 Proficiency Bonus
You know how to hide everything even your heartbeat. While using the [[Hold Your Breath]] Action you can also choose to stop your heart, letting you bypass any life senses.

### Perception Proficiency
You gain the following benefits when having a Proficiency Bonus equal to or higher than the given value.

#### +1 Proficiency Bonus
You gain the [[Heighten Senses]] Action. If you already have the Heighten Senses Action increase the bonus gained from the Action by 1. 

#### +2 Proficiency Bonus
Your acute senses allow you to pick up things from larger distances. Double the range needed to incur range specific Perception DC Increases. Example is the `+1 per meter` from `Distance to the Source (Hearing)` Perception Modifier would instead be `+1 per 2 meters`.

#### +3 Proficiency Bonus
You remain alert to sound even in your sleep. Ignore the DC increase from being asleep.

#### +4 Proficiency Bonus
You can [[Skills#Take 10 and 20 | Take 10]] on Perception Rolls during an Encounter.

#### +5 Proficiency Bonus
Your precise senses always warn you of danger. When [[Combat#Rolling Initiative| rolling for initiative]] you can Roll a Perception Roll instead of an Initiative Roll.
