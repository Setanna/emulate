# Design Philosophy
How Emulate is designed.

##### Resources
Resources are an important piece of any game design. How often can something be done, how powerful is it and how is it done, are all things that can be answered by resources:
- How many resources do you have left?
- How many resources do you dump into it?
- How do you regain or do you have the resources?

Resources can be tweaked and used in many ways, such as how to gain resources or how to use them.

<br>

**Gaining resources**
The most common is that you have an amount of resources that you regain every rest. But there are many ways to gain resources such as:
- *Rage:* Whenever you deal or take physical damage
- *Ki:* Regain resources at end of turn or when using Catch your Breath Action

These resources have ways of gaining them that are narrative fitting with the mechanical use of them.

<br>

**Unique Resources**
An often overlooked thing, is that some things that are often not considered resources, can be used as resources. This can be things such as Hit Points or even time:
- *??:* Expend Hit Points to get an effect
- *Strength of Words:* Increase Rally effect over each turn

This can allow abilities to have very unique mechanical and narrative flair.



### Design Notes
- Flavor and Mechanics should always go hand in hand
- X times per day should only exist in the most rare of cases
	- Such as Spells
- Time is a Resource
	- Refer to Strength of Words
- Anything that does not weaken is absolute
	- Armor does not weaken, so the damage reduction is absolute
- If everyone had this would Encounters break?
	- Alternatively is this ability a must have?
- Power is not just about numbers
	- Power is about frequency, access and recovery
- Snowballing mechanics must be interruptible
- Passive power is invisible power
